1. Introduction

komaru.dev (the “Developer”) respects the privacy of people using Ninja Ping (the “App”). This policy describes how information is handled in the App.

2. Information Entered by Users

The App lets users enter projects, tasks, steps, names of contacts, notes, and attached images. This information is generally stored on the user’s device. The Developer does not operate its own server to collect this information.

3. Images and Attachments

Images selected by users are stored on the device as App data. The App does not automatically upload these images to the Developer’s servers. Image selection may use system features provided by Apple.

4. Data Storage and Backups

The App uses technologies such as SwiftData and UserDefaults to store information. The App does not provide its own cloud synchronization feature. Depending on device settings, App data may be included in backups provided by Apple.

5. Advertising

The current version of the App does not include third-party ad delivery. If advertising services are added in the future, this policy will be updated as needed, and appropriate notices or consent requests will be provided.

6. In-App Purchases

The current version of the App does not include in-app purchases. If introduced in the future, purchases are expected to use payment systems provided by Apple or similar services.

7. Analytics and Tracking

The current version of the App does not include developer-operated analytics that send user behavior off-device or tracking for advertising purposes. Diagnostic and crash information provided by Apple may be handled according to the user’s device settings and Apple’s services.

8. Sharing Information with Third Parties

The Developer does not collect user-entered projects or notes on its own servers and does not sell that information to third parties. This does not cover information users choose to share or export themselves.

9. Deleting Data

Users can delete information they entered in the App. However, some related information may remain in separate areas of App storage. Uninstalling the App generally removes locally stored App data, but copies may remain in backups provided by Apple.

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended to intentionally collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

The Developer may update this policy to reflect changes in the App or applicable laws. Updates will be published on this page.
